## Architecture and project

1. **What does the application do?** It combines public travel discovery and reservations with staff operations for bookings, assignments, hospitality and business administration. **Code:** [`server/app.js`](../../server/app.js), [`server/server.js`](../../server/server.js), [`server/routes/index.js`](../../server/routes/index.js), [`server/models/`](../../server/models).
2. **What is the high-level architecture?** React/Vite SPA → Express 5 API → Mongoose models → MongoDB, with provider integrations and optional Socket.IO/background work. **Code:** [`server/app.js`](../../server/app.js), [`server/server.js`](../../server/server.js), [`server/routes/index.js`](../../server/routes/index.js), [`server/models/`](../../server/models).
3. **Why split client and server?** They have independent entry points, dependencies and build/deploy configuration; the browser communicates through HTTP/API modules. **Code:** [`server/app.js`](../../server/app.js), [`server/server.js`](../../server/server.js), [`server/routes/index.js`](../../server/routes/index.js), [`server/models/`](../../server/models).
4. **Is this strict MVC?** No. It has route/controller/service/model layers, but responsibilities are distributed and some controllers/models have substantial domain logic. **Code:** [`server/app.js`](../../server/app.js), [`server/server.js`](../../server/server.js), [`server/routes/index.js`](../../server/routes/index.js), [`server/models/`](../../server/models).
5. **What is the server entry point?** `server/server.js`; it starts HTTP/Socket.IO, initializes DB/readiness, starts background services and handles shutdown. **Code:** [`server/app.js`](../../server/app.js), [`server/server.js`](../../server/server.js), [`server/routes/index.js`](../../server/routes/index.js), [`server/models/`](../../server/models).
6. **What does `app.js` own?** Express middleware order, CORS, health/readiness, route mounting, 404 and error responses. **Code:** [`server/app.js`](../../server/app.js), [`server/server.js`](../../server/server.js), [`server/routes/index.js`](../../server/routes/index.js), [`server/models/`](../../server/models).
7. **Why are there readiness and liveness endpoints?** Liveness says the process responds; readiness also requires successful startup and Mongo connection. **Code:** [`server/app.js`](../../server/app.js), [`server/server.js`](../../server/server.js), [`server/routes/index.js`](../../server/routes/index.js), [`server/models/`](../../server/models).
8. **What runs after database startup?** Index migration and scheduled/work-queue services, including lifecycle, retention, communications and compliance tasks. **Code:** [`server/app.js`](../../server/app.js), [`server/server.js`](../../server/server.js), [`server/routes/index.js`](../../server/routes/index.js), [`server/models/`](../../server/models).
9. **What is the system's primary external dependency?** MongoDB is a hard dependency for normal API work; payment and other integrations are feature-specific dependencies. **Code:** [`server/app.js`](../../server/app.js), [`server/server.js`](../../server/server.js), [`server/routes/index.js`](../../server/routes/index.js), [`server/models/`](../../server/models).
10. **Does the repo prove production deployment?** No. Render/Vercel manifests describe configuration; deployed state requires host-side evidence. **Code:** [`server/app.js`](../../server/app.js), [`server/server.js`](../../server/server.js), [`server/routes/index.js`](../../server/routes/index.js), [`server/models/`](../../server/models).
